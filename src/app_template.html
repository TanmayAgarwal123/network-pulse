    How priorities and reminders are worked out

    Who is tracked: everyone, unless there is a concrete reason not to. Set aside are people whose employer or title points to India, staffing agencies, and rows LinkedIn exported with no name. People you connected with in 2024 or earlier, with nothing confirming where they are now, are marked "location unverified" and tracked only from priority 30 up.

    Priority (0–100): seniority counts on its own. Executives, directors, principals, staff engineers, managers and founders score high even with no message history. Employer relevance to AI/LLM roles, in-house recruiters, AI/ML titles and a real two-way thread add to it.

    How often: priority 70 and up comes due every 7 days, everyone else every 30. Re-evaluate with AI can shorten an interval but never push it past 30. A weekly touch does not have to be a direct message: a comment on their post or a shared article counts, and you can log it the same way.

    Location is a guess. LinkedIn's export has no location field. Use the location filter and the "Mark as…" button on a person to correct it.

    Where your activity lives: in this page's own database on Claude, which keeps it across visits, devices and app updates. A compact copy is also saved to your Google Drive every Sunday evening.
